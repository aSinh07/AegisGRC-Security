CVSS Guide

CVSS is vulnerability severity, not a company score.

0.0 None · 0.1–3.9 Low · 4.0–6.9 Medium · 7.0–8.9 High · 9.0–10.0 Critical. There is no universal CVSS a company must achieve. Prioritize remediation using severity together with exploitability, asset criticality and business impact.
